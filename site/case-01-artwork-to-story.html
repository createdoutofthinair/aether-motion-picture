One song, two worlds

In the opening plaza the two dancers can see the same horizon and still feel alone. The city gives that distance a physical scale. On the first chorus the glass corridor gives way to an open, reflective stage under a black sun. The change in space makes the lift in the music visible.

Map the tension before designing frames

Play the master with the timed phrases from ElevenLabs. Mark where a breath, a new instrumental layer or a change of register actually shifts the feeling. The song stays at 71 BPM. The chorus dancers sometimes take two counts to a beat, giving the choreography a 142 BPM subdivision at natural speed.

Hold onto the same people

Classify the front, profile, three-quarter and full-body references for each performer. Keep her black tank, his black long-sleeve crewneck and both pairs of bare feet consistent across worlds. Check reflections as carefully as the figures. Shot 02 needed a pickup when a coat and shoes appeared in the walking shot.
