Give a three-second shot an emotional turn

On this pageBuild a reverse that cuts togetherGive the model a single actionGive the editor room to choose

A facial insert can carry a lyric with only a glance. The HARROW reserve shots use the same cast sheets as the wide choreography, but each has a different starting expression and a visible change.

Build a reverse that cuts together

In RSV-01 she looks down, then lifts wet eyes to screen right. In RSV-02 he looks left and the tension in his jaw gives way to a smile. That opposite screen direction allows the editor to place the two shots together as a look and a reply.

Give the model a single action

The third frame starts with a sliver of eclipse light between their profiles. In the motion take their foreheads meet and their eyes close. Assign the opening image to composition and lighting. Assign the two cast references to identity. Keep both profiles readable as the gap closes.

Give the editor room to choose

All three reserves were delivered separately as silent 1920 × 1080 MP4s. Each contains 72 frames at 24 fps. They can be laid over the clean v04 picture while the song continues underneath. “You’re not alone” is one possible place for the duet.
