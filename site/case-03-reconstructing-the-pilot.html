Repair the movement at the cut

On this pageMake weight readableWatch the last secondRecord the result you actually gotHand off the edit

A still can be beautiful while the movement it starts feels stranded. The review copy puts a placement number and source filename over the moving picture so the artist can identify the exact moment.

Make weight readable

At placement 30 the original feet appeared to skate. In the pickup both dancers start close to the camera and drive diagonally away. A fixed wide frame, floor seams and receding figures make displacement visible. Watch the supporting foot through its push-off, then check how far the body has traveled.

Watch the last second

Shot 07 originally settled before the next cut. The selected replacement keeps traveling through a turn. In the closing shot the opposite choice works: the camera holds while the couple continues to walk away through the final frame.

Record the result you actually got

At 3:05.46, shot 32 begins at the dancers’ feet and widens as directed. The generated reveal shifts to a plain studio wall. That visible continuity issue remains in v04 and belongs in the review record.

Hand off the edit

The cut contains 35 placements over 5,123 frames. Its three scene dissolves overlap source material. Preserve the source trim and the timeline position separately; each placement in the archive links its editorial number to a real source basename.
